## Track Overview

The Education track is designed for current and aspiring educators, instructional designers, and learning professionals — covering pedagogy, curriculum design, learning assessment, and the organizational and policy context in which education happens.

**Target audience:**
- K–12 teachers and school administrators seeking formal competency documentation
- Corporate learning and development professionals
- Instructional designers working in higher education or ed-tech

**Important note:** This track does not satisfy state teaching licensure requirements. It documents competency in educational practice — it is not a substitute for required licensure pathways in jurisdictions where a teaching license is legally required.

## This Track Needs a Maintainer

Building a track means:
1. Defining 8–12 competency domains with clear, specific standards
2. Identifying 3+ high-quality courses per domain
3. Specifying Tier 1/2/3 assessment options for each domain
4. Documenting PLA examples for working educators
5. Maintaining course links as the landscape evolves

**If you want to build this track:**
1. Open a GitHub issue with label `new-track`
2. Include: your proposed competency list, your relevant background, and your maintenance commitment
3. See the full process in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#2-how-to-propose-a-new-track)
