## Track Overview

The Computer Science track is designed for self-directed learners, career changers, and professionals seeking a rigorous, verifiable CS credential that demonstrates practical software engineering competency — covering both theoretical foundations and applied development skills.

**Target audience:**
- Software engineers who built skills through bootcamps, self-study, or professional experience without a formal CS degree
- Career changers entering the software industry
- Technical professionals seeking formal competency documentation

## This Track Needs a Maintainer

Building a track means:
1. Defining 8–12 competency domains with clear, specific standards
2. Identifying 3+ high-quality courses per domain
3. Specifying Tier 1/2/3 assessment options for each domain
4. Documenting PLA examples for professional developers
5. Maintaining course links as the landscape evolves

**If you want to build this track:**
1. Open a GitHub issue with label `new-track`
2. Include: your proposed competency list, your relevant background, and your maintenance commitment
3. See the full process in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#2-how-to-propose-a-new-track)
