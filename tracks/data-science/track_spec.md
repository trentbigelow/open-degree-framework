## Track Overview

The Data Science track is designed for analysts, engineers, and researchers who work with data at scale — covering statistical reasoning, machine learning, data engineering, and the communication of data-driven insights to non-technical audiences.

**Target audience:**
- Data analysts and business intelligence professionals seeking formal ML competency documentation
- Engineers transitioning into data science roles
- Researchers applying statistical methods in professional contexts

---

## Proposed Competency Domains

*Draft list — not finalized. Subject to change through the track drafting process.*

- Statistical Inference & Probability
- Machine Learning — Supervised Methods
- Machine Learning — Unsupervised & Reinforcement Methods
- Data Engineering & Pipelines
- Data Visualization & Communication
- SQL & Database Querying
- Python or R for Data Analysis
- Experiment Design & A/B Testing
- Applied Domain Specialization (e.g., NLP, Computer Vision, Time Series)

**Proposed electives (not yet defined):** Deep Learning, MLOps, Causal Inference, Bayesian Methods

## This Track Needs a Maintainer

Building a track means:
1. Defining 8–12 competency domains with clear, specific standards
2. Identifying 3+ high-quality courses per domain
3. Specifying Tier 1/2/3 assessment options for each domain
4. Documenting PLA examples for practicing data professionals
5. Maintaining course links as the landscape evolves

**If you want to build this track:**
1. Open a GitHub issue with label `new-track`
2. Include: your proposed competency list, your relevant background, and your maintenance commitment
3. See the full process in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#2-how-to-propose-a-new-track)
