## Track Overview

The Healthcare Administration track is designed for professionals working in healthcare operations, policy, and management — covering the regulatory landscape, healthcare finance, quality improvement, and the operational management of healthcare organizations.

**Target audience:**
- Clinical professionals transitioning into administrative or leadership roles
- Healthcare operations staff seeking formal management competency documentation
- Policy professionals working in health systems

**Important note:** This track covers healthcare administration and management — not clinical practice. It does not satisfy requirements for licensed clinical credentials (medicine, nursing, pharmacy, etc.).
