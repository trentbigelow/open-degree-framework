# Maintainers

> Maintainers are responsible for the health and integrity of the Open Degree Framework. They review pull requests, approve curriculum changes, oversee the appeals process, and ensure the framework evolves with the learning landscape.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

As defined in [FRAMEWORK.md §9](FRAMEWORK.md#9-governance--appeals), maintainers are responsible for:

- **Pull request review** — reviewing and merging contributions to the framework spec, track specs, and supporting documentation
- **Track approval** — approving new specialization track proposals (2 approvals required)
- **Curriculum oversight** — ensuring course recommendations remain current and high-quality
- **Appeals** — participating in PLA and capstone appeal panels when assigned
- **Community health** — upholding the code of conduct and contribution standards

---

## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainership is earned through sustained, high-quality contribution to the project.

**Path to maintainership:**
1. Make 3+ meaningful pull request contributions (not just typo fixes) that are merged
2. Demonstrate domain expertise in at least one ODF track or the core framework
3. Be nominated by an existing maintainer
4. Receive approval from 2 existing maintainers

**What maintainers need:**
- Time: approximately 2–4 hours per month for routine maintenance
- Domain knowledge: enough to evaluate curriculum quality in at least one track
- Judgment: ability to apply framework principles consistently across diverse PRs

If you're interested in becoming a maintainer, engage actively in issues and pull requests. We'll notice.

---

## Maintainer Governance

**Quorum:** Any decision requiring maintainer approval needs a simple majority of active maintainers (minimum 2 when the team has 3 or more).

**Inactivity:** A maintainer who has not reviewed a PR or participated in a governance decision in 90 days is considered inactive. Inactive maintainers retain their status but are not counted for quorum. An inactive maintainer may be removed by a 2/3 vote of active maintainers after a 14-day notice period.

**Removal:** A maintainer may be removed for violation of the code of conduct, sustained inactivity (see above), or by their own request. Removal requires a 2/3 vote of remaining active maintainers.

---

## Long-Term Governance

The current model — GitHub maintainers operating under CC BY 4.0 — is the founding structure. The long-term goal is an independent Open Degree Foundation with a formal community governance model.

If you want to help build that structure, open an issue with label `governance` and let's talk.
